## Chapter 1: Before You Go

### How to use this guide

This guide is built for one thing: helping you see Mt. Fuji on a day trip from Tokyo, without wasting time on confusing transport or bad timing.

- **Chapter 2** shows you exactly how to get there and back.
- **Chapter 3** covers the 8 places worth your time.
- **Chapter 4** gives you ready-made itineraries you can follow as-is.
- **Chapters 5–6** cover food, manners and the small things that make the day go smoothly.

Every place in this guide is in the Fuji Five Lakes area (Yamanashi Prefecture), the easiest Fuji area to reach from Tokyo.

### When can you actually see Mt. Fuji?

Mt. Fuji hides behind clouds more often than most visitors expect. Timing matters more than anything else.

| Season | Chance of a clear view | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Winter (Dec–Feb) | Best | Cold, dry air. Snow-capped peak. |
| Spring (Mar–May) | Good | Cherry blossoms in April. Haze increases in May. |
| Summer (Jun–Aug) | Lowest | Rainy season and humidity. Peak often hidden. |
| Autumn (Sep–Nov) | Good | Red leaves in November. Typhoons possible in September. |

**Time of day:** Mornings are clearest. Clouds usually build up from late morning. Aim to arrive by 9–10 a.m.

### What if Fuji is hidden?

1. **Check the live cameras the night before and the morning of your trip.** [CHECK: confirm a reliable live camera site to recommend]
2. **Have a flexible date.** If you have several days in Tokyo, keep the Fuji day movable and pick the clearest forecast.
3. **Use the rainy-day plan in Chapter 4.** The area still has great food, hot springs and museums.

## Chapter 2: Getting There from Tokyo

### Option A: Train

The **Fuji Excursion** limited express runs directly from **Shinjuku Station** to **Kawaguchiko Station**.

- Time: about 1 hour 54–58 minutes, no transfers
- Fare: ¥4,200 one way for adults (ticket ¥2,580 + express fee ¥1,620). To Shimoyoshida (for Chureito Pagoda): ¥4,010. (Fujikyu Railway and JR East official sites, checked November 2026.)
- Stops: Shinjuku → Tachikawa → Hachioji → Otsuki → Tsuru-Bunkadaigakumae → **Shimoyoshida (Chureito Pagoda)** → Mt. Fuji → **Fuji-Q Highland** → Kawaguchiko
- Only **4 round trips a day** (plus 1 extra on some dates). There are no non-reserved cars: buy a reserved seat, or a "seat-unspecified" ticket.
- Tickets go on sale **one month before, at 10 a.m.** Book on **"JR-EAST Train Reservation"** (the English booking site for overseas visitors) or Eki-net. Morning trains can be almost full even on weekdays.
- **Important:** The Fuji Excursion is only 3 cars. Between Shinjuku and Otsuki it is coupled to another express train ("Azusa" or "Kaiji"). **Make sure you board Cars 1–3**, or you will end up going somewhere else.

**Weekday timetable (from March 14, 2026, Fujikyu official site):**

|---|---|---|

| Fuji Excursion 11 | 9:30 | 11:28 |
| Fuji Excursion 15 | 10:30 | 12:24 |

| To Tokyo | Leave Kawaguchiko | Arrive Shinjuku |
|---|---|---|

Weekend times are different. Always check the latest timetable before booking.

**JR Pass users:** the nationwide Japan Rail Pass covers Shinjuku–Otsuki only. From Otsuki to Kawaguchiko (Fujikyu Railway) you pay extra: fare ¥1,170 + express fee ¥600 (children ¥400) = ¥1,770 one way. Getting off at Shimoyoshida for Chureito Pagoda: fare ¥980 + express fee ¥600 = ¥1,580. (Fujikyu Railway official fare and fee tables, checked November 2026.) Children pay half the fare (Otsuki–Kawaguchiko ¥590). Suica / PASMO work on the Fujikyu Railway, and the IC card fare is a little cheaper (Otsuki–Kawaguchiko ¥1,161). You can buy the Fujikyu express ticket before boarding or on the train; the price is the same ¥600 either way. If you have no time to buy it, you can pay the difference at the station where you get off. (Confirmed by phone with the Fujikyu call center, November 2, 2026.)

**JR TOKYO Wide Pass users:** this 3-day pass (for non-Japanese passport holders only) covers the **whole Fujikyu Railway**, so the Fuji Excursion is covered all the way from Shinjuku to Kawaguchiko. Get your free seat reservation at a JR ticket machine or ticket office **before boarding**; without one you may only use empty seats. Extra fees still apply for reserved seats on Fujikyu's own "Fujisan Express" and "Fujisan View Express", and for the "Fuji Tozan Densha" sightseeing train. Carry your original passport while using the pass. (JR East official terms of use, as of March 2026.)

**Backup option:** If the Fuji Excursion is full, take the "Kaiji" limited express from Shinjuku to **Otsuki**, then change to Fujikyu Railway's own trains to Kawaguchiko. It takes about 20 minutes longer, and the Fujikyu section cannot be booked on Eki-net. On the Fujikyu section you pay the fare (¥1,170) plus, on Fujikyu's own express trains, an express fee of ¥400 (Otsuki–Kawaguchiko). The "Fujisan View Express" also charges ¥200 for a reserved seat in Car 2 or ¥900 for the special Car 1; the "Fujisan Express" charges ¥200 for a reserved seat in Cars 1–2. Note: these express fees are different from the Fuji Excursion's ¥600. (Fujikyu Railway official fee table, checked November 2026.)

**Which side to see Mt. Fuji (Fujikyu Railway section, Otsuki → Kawaguchiko):** the view switches sides, so there is no single "best side".

| Section | Mt. Fuji is on the |
|---|---|
| Otsuki → Tsukiedera | Left side (facing the direction of travel) |
| Tsukiedera → Mt. Fuji Station | Right side |
| Mt. Fuji Station → Kawaguchiko | Left side |

On the way back to Tokyo it is the opposite. (Fujikyu Railway official Q&A, checked November 2026.)


### Option B: Highway bus

Buses leave from **Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal ("Busta Shinjuku")** and arrive at **Kawaguchiko Station**.

- Time: about 1 hour 45 minutes to Kawaguchiko Station (about 1 hour 40 minutes to Fuji-Q Highland). Traffic can make it longer.
- Fare: ¥2,000–2,200 one way for adults; the lower price is the online discount ("Net-wari"). Standard fare ¥2,200. No round-trip discount. (Highway Bus.com official pages, checked November 2026.)
- The same bus also stops at **Fuji-Q Highland** (same fare), and continues to Oshino Hakkai and Lake Yamanakako. In summer some buses go up to the Mt. Fuji 5th Station.
- Frequency: every 10 minutes to 1 hour
- **No rest stops** on the way, but there is a **toilet on board**. (Fujikyu Bus official FAQ, checked November 2026.)
- **Cancellation:** you can cancel until the bus departs. The fee is ¥110 per one-way ticket (it can differ by route or ticket type). Cancel on the website if you booked online, or at the counter if you bought there. With a round-trip booking you can cancel just one direction. (Fujikyu Bus official FAQ, checked November 2026.)
- **If you miss the bus:** highway buses leave exactly on time. Once the bus has left, your ticket is **void**: no change to a later bus and no refund. If you might be late, change or cancel **before** departure (online, by phone, or at the counter). (Fujikyu Bus official FAQ)
- **Luggage:** one suitcase per person goes free of charge in the luggage space under the bus. (Fujikyu Bus official FAQ)
- **Children:** elementary school age and under pay half the adult fare. One preschool child per adult rides free if they sit on your lap; a child who takes their own seat pays the child fare. (Fujikyu Bus official FAQ)
- You can choose a window or aisle seat when booking, and there are women-only seats.
- Pay online by credit card, or buy at the ticket counters at Busta Shinjuku, Fuji-Q Highland, Kawaguchiko Station and Mt. Fuji Station.
- All seats reserved. Tickets go on sale **one month before, at 10:00 a.m.** After booking online you must pay (credit card, convenience store, etc.) by the deadline shown, or the booking is cancelled automatically. (Fujikyu Bus official FAQ) Weekend buses sell out.
- **English help:** the main booking sites (such as Fujiyama Connect) work in English, Chinese and Korean, and the bus ticket counters at Kawaguchiko Station and Mt. Fuji Station may have English- or Chinese-speaking staff. Fujikyu Call Center: 0570-022956. (Fujikyu Bus official FAQ)

### Train or bus? Quick comparison

| | Train (Fuji Excursion) | Highway bus |
|---|---|---|
| Time | About 2 hours, reliable | About 2 hours, traffic-dependent |
| Price | Higher | Lower |
| Comfort | More legroom, can walk around | Seat belt required. No rest stops, toilet on board. |
| Best for | Tight schedules, return trip on busy days | Budget travelers, weekday trips |

**Our recommendation:** Highway buses can be delayed when the roads are busy. If you must be back in Tokyo by a fixed time, the train is the safer choice for the return trip.

### Getting around: the sightseeing buses

From Kawaguchiko Station, retro-style sightseeing buses loop around the lakes.

| Line | Main stops | Useful for |
|---|---|---|
| Red Line (Kawaguchiko area) | Ropeway ("Yuransen / Ropeway Iriguchi" stop), Kawaguchiko Music Forest Museum, Itchiku Kubota Art Museum, Oishi Park ("Kawaguchiko Natural Living Center" stop) | Chapter 3: places 2 and 4 |
| Green Line (Lake Saiko area) | Lake Saiko, Saiko Iyashi-no-Sato Nenba, Saiko Bat Cave, Fugaku Wind Cave (last stop) | Chapter 3: place 5 |
| Blue Line (Narusawa / Lake Shojiko / Lake Motosu) | Mt. Fuji World Heritage Center, Narusawa Ice Cave, Fugaku Wind Cave, Lake Shojiko ("Kodaki Fuji View Point"), Lake Motosu | Chapter 3: place 8 |

- **Bus passes** ("Kawaguchiko, Saiko, Motosuko Sightseeing Coupon"): unlimited rides on all three lines (Red, Green, Blue) and on regular route buses inside the area (except the Fuji Shibazakura shuttle bus).

| Pass | Adults (junior high and up) | Children (elementary school) |
|---|---|---|
| 1-Day Pass | ¥1,500 | ¥750 |
| 2-Day Pass | ¥2,000 | ¥1,000 |
| 2-Day Pass + Panoramic Ropeway round trip + Lake Kawaguchiko cruise (or Oishi Park boat line one way) | ¥3,300 | ¥1,650 |

  Buy at the bus ticket office at Kawaguchiko Station, or online on Fujikyu Bus's "Fujiyama Connect" site (online sales close at 12:00 noon on the day of use). The 2-Day Pass is valid for two days from the date you choose. Online cancellation is free until the day before; on the day, the full price is charged. (Fujikyu Bus official site and Fujiyama Connect, checked November 2026.)
- **Single fares on the Red Line (adults):** Kawaguchiko Station to the Ropeway ¥180, to Itchiku Kubota Art Museum ¥440, to Oishi Park (Kawaguchiko Natural Living Center) ¥570. Ropeway to Oishi Park ¥470. (Fujikyu Bus official fare table, checked November 2026.)

(Fujikyu Bus official fare table, checked November 2026.) The "Motosuko" stop is also ¥1,470 (confirmed by phone with Fujikyu Bus, November 2, 2026).

Fuji World Heritage Loop Bus, regular route buses in the Five Lakes area, **and** the Fujikyu Railway. It also gives discounts at sightseeing spots. There are two types:

## Know Before You Go

These are the points that most often catch travelers out, and that quick online answers often get wrong or leave out. We checked each one with the operators themselves in November 2026. Details are in the chapters shown.

| | What to know | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fuji Excursion train: board Cars 1–3 only.** The other cars go somewhere else. | 2 |
| 2 | **Japan Rail Pass covers Shinjuku–Otsuki only.** Otsuki to Kawaguchiko costs ¥1,770 extra (fare + express fee). Children's express fee on this section is ¥400. | 2 |
| 3 | **Miss your highway bus and the ticket is void.** No later bus, no refund. Change or cancel before departure. | 2 |
| 4 | **No big suitcases on the sightseeing buses.** Use the coin lockers right outside Kawaguchiko Station's ticket gates (¥700–1,200). | 2 |
| 5 | **Red Line only? Don't buy the 1-Day Pass.** Single tickets are cheaper. Buy it if you ride the Green or Blue Line. | 2 |
| 6 | **Chureito Pagoda is about 25 minutes on foot from Shimoyoshida Station,** including 398 steps. | 3 |
| 7 | **Oshino Hakkai: you may touch the water, but never throw coins in.** | 3 |
| 8 | **The ¥1,000-bill view at Lake Motosu is about 1 hour on foot from the bus stop.** Buses run about once an hour; last bus back 16:45. | 3 |
| 9 | **Fuji-Q Highland has closing days, and its Ferris wheel is closed until January 31, 2027.** | 3 |
| 10 | **Mt. Fuji is often hidden in summer.** In 2025 the whole mountain was visible at 8 a.m. on only 2 days in June. | 1 |
| 11 | **Tattoos:** Fuji Choubou no Yu Yurari says no; Royal Hotel Kawaguchiko says yes. | 4 |
| 12 | **The Kawaguchiko Music Forest Museum is closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.** | 4 |
